The 10 Most Famous Mathematicians:
1. Carl Friedrich Gauss - Prince of mathematicians, Disquisitiones Arithmeticae, Gaussian distribution, number theory contributions
2. Leonhard Euler - Notation f(x), e constant (~2.718), Euler's formula e^(iπ)+1=0, graph theory foundations, prolific output
3. Isaac Newton - Calculus (fluxions), binomial theorem, optics, law of universal gravitation, co-inventor of calculus
4. Pythagoras - Pythagorean theorem a²+b²=c², number theory, Pythagorean school, mathematical rigor
5. Bernhard Riemann - Riemannian geometry, Riemann hypothesis ( Millennium Prize Problem), zeta function, manifold theory
6. David Hilbert - Hilbert spaces, 23 foundational problems for 20th century mathematics, formalism, invariant theory
7. Alan Turing - Turing machine, computability theory, "On Computable Numbers," codebreaking at Bletchley Park
8. Joseph Fourier - Fourier series, heat equation, harmonic analysis, greenhouse effect discovery
9. René Descartes - Cartesian coordinate system, analytic geometry, "I think therefore I am" philosopher/mathematician
10. Euclid - Elements, geometric axioms, Euclidean geometry framework, mathematical proof structure

Foundational Impact: Gauss and Euler laid much of the groundwork for modern number theory and analysis; Turing established the theoretical basis for computer science.
