The 10 Most Famous Nietzschean Ideas:
1. God is Dead - Diagnosis of modern nihilism after the collapse of the religious worldview
2. Will to Power - Fundamental driving force of life, self-overcoming, and creativity
3. Ubermensch - The individual who creates own values beyond herd morality
4. Eternal Recurrence - Thought experiment: would you live this life infinitely again?
5. Master vs Slave Morality - Value-creating vs resentment-based ethics in the Genealogy
6. Amor Fati - Love of fate; embracing all of one's existence
7. Thus Spoke Zarathustra - Philosophical novel presenting his mature doctrine poetically
8. Perspectivism - "There are no facts, only interpretations"
9. Apollonian vs Dionysian - Order vs ecstasy in art and culture (Birth of Tragedy)
10. Revaluation of All Values - Call to rebuild ethics from life-affirmation upward
