The 10 Most Famous Einsteinian Contributions:
1. Special Relativity - Space and time unify into spacetime; constant speed of light (1905)
2. General Relativity - Gravity as curvature of spacetime (1915), confirmed by 1919 eclipse
3. Mass-Energy Equivalence - E=mc², basis of nuclear physics
4. Photoelectric Effect - Light quanta explanation, Nobel Prize 1921, seed of quantum theory
5. Brownian Motion Proof - Quantitative evidence for atoms' existence (1905)
6. Miracle Year 1905 - Four revolutionary papers while a patent clerk
7. Bose-Einstein Statistics - Prediction of condensate states, confirmed 1995
8. EPR Paradox - Quantum entanglement challenge later verified experimentally
9. Cosmological Constant - Introduced then abandoned, revived for dark energy
10. Public Science Advocate - Pacifism, civil rights, and popular relativity exposition
