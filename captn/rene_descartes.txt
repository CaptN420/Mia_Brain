The 10 Most Famous Cartesian Contributions:
1. Cogito Ergo Sum - "I think, therefore I am" as indubitable foundation of knowledge
2. Methodic Doubt - Doubt everything to find what cannot be doubted
3. Cartesian Coordinates - Merged algebra and geometry; foundation of analytic geometry
4. Mind-Body Dualism - Distinct substances of thought and extension
5. Discourse on Method - Four rules for certain knowledge (1637)
6. Meditations - Systematic metaphysics through six meditations (1641)
7. Mechanistic Physics - Universe as matter in motion; vortex theory
8. Innate Ideas - Certain concepts exist prior to experience
9. Pineal Gland Theory - Site of mind-body interaction
10. Father of Modern Philosophy - Set agenda of epistemology for centuries
