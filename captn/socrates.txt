The 10 Most Famous Elements of Socratic Thought:
1. Socratic Method - Question-and-answer dialectic exposing contradictions to reach truth
2. Know Thyself - Delphic maxim made the core of philosophical life
3. Intellectual Humility - "I know that I know nothing" as the starting point of wisdom
4. Ethics over Metaphysics - Shifted philosophy from nature-study to how to live well
5. The Unexamined Life - "The unexamined life is not worth living" at his trial
6. Areté (Virtue) - Virtue is knowledge; no one does wrong willingly
7. Maieutics - Midwifery of ideas: helping others give birth to their own understanding
8. Civil Obedience - Refused escape from prison, accepting Athens' verdict in Crito
9. Trial of 399 BC - Condemned for impiety and corrupting youth; became martyr of free thought
10. Platonic Legacy - Wrote nothing himself; preserved through Plato's dialogues
