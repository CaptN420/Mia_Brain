The 10 Most Famous Scientists:
1. Albert Einstein - Theory of relativity, E=mc², photoelectric effect, Nobel Prize 1921
2. Isaac Newton - Laws of motion, universal gravitation, calculus, optics
3. Charles Darwin - Theory of evolution by natural selection, On the Origin of Species
4. Nikola Tesla - Alternating current systems, Tesla coil, electromagnetism innovations
5. Galileo Galilei - Telescopic astronomy, heliocentrism, scientific method, kinematics
6. Marie Curie - Radioactivity (coined the term), Nobel Prizes in Physics (1903) and Chemistry (1911)
7. Stephen Hawking - Black holes, singularities, Hawking radiation, best-selling author
8. Thomas Edison - Light bulb practical implementation, phonograph, 1,093 patents held
9. James Clerk Maxwell - Electromagnetic theory, Maxwell's equations, color theory
10. Louis Pasteur - Germ theory of disease, pasteurization, vaccination principles

Notable Mentions: Nikola Tesla's contributions to AC power systems revolutionized electrical distribution; Marie Curie remains the only person to win Nobel Prizes in two different scientific fields.
