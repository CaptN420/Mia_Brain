The 10 Most Famous Lockean Contributions:
1. Tabula Rasa - Mind at birth as blank slate shaped by experience
2. Natural Rights - Life, liberty, property as inherent rights
3. Two Treatises of Government - Justification of revolution against tyranny (1689)
4. Social Contract - Government exists by consent of the governed
5. Separation of Powers - Early legislative/executive division influencing Montesquieu
6. Religious Tolerance - Letter Concerning Toleration; church-state separation
7. Empiricism - All knowledge derives from sensation and reflection
8. Right of Rebellion - People may overthrow governments violating the trust
9. Property Theory - Labor mixing creates legitimate ownership
10. Influence on Founders - Direct source for US Declaration of Independence
