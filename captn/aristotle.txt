The 10 Most Famous Aristotelian Contributions:
1. Formal Logic - Syllogistic reasoning in the Organon; foundation of logic for 2,000 years
2. Lyceum Academy - Founded 335 BC; peripatetic school of systematic research
3. Tutor of Alexander - Educated Alexander the Great in philosophy and statecraft
4. Nicomachean Ethics - Eudaimonia (flourishing) through virtue and the golden mean
5. Politics - "Man is by nature a political animal"; comparative constitutions
6. Empirical Biology - Classified 500+ animal species by direct observation
7. Four Causes - Material, formal, efficient, final causation framework
8. Poetics - Catharsis theory, dramatic structure rules still used today
9. Physics and Cosmology - Geocentric motion theory dominant until Newton
10. Metaphysics - Substance, potentiality and actuality; "first philosophy"
