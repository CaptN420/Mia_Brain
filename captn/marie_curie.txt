1. Marie Curie - Discovered radium and polonium through painstaking isolation from tons of uranium ore, pioneering radiochemistry.
2. Marie Curie - Formulated the theory of radioactivity as an atomic property, coining the term "radioactive."
3. Marie Curie - Discovered that radioactivity is a nuclear phenomenon independent of chemical bonding or physical state.
4. Marie Curie - Developed precise measurement techniques for radioactive emissions using piezoelectric electrometry.
5. Marie Curie - Established the principle of radioactive half-life and the exponential law of radioactive decay.
6. Marie Curie - Isolated pure radium metal for the first time, establishing radiochemistry as a distinct discipline.
7. Marie Curie - Developed the method of fractional crystallization for separating rare radioactive elements from complex mixtures.
8. Marie Curie - Discovered that radium emits three distinct types of radiation (alpha, beta, gamma) with different penetrating powers.
9. Marie Curie - Established the unit of radioactivity (curie) and absolute measurement standards for radiation.
10. Marie Curie - Founded the Radium Institute (Institut du Radium) which became a world center for nuclear physics and chemistry.
11. Marie Curie - Pioneered the medical application of radium for cancer therapy, founding radiation oncology.
12. Marie Curie - Discovered that thorium compounds emit radiation, proving radioactivity is widespread in nature.
13. Marie Curie - Developed mobile radiography units ("Little Curies") during World War I, bringing X-ray diagnostics to battlefields.
14. Marie Curie - Established that radioactive decay involves transformation of elements, providing first evidence for transmutation.
15. Marie Curie - Determined the atomic weight of radium (226.0) through painstaking chemical purification and crystallography.
