The 10 Most Famous Confucian Teachings:
1. Ren (Benevolence) - Humaneness as supreme virtue governing all relationships
2. Li (Ritual Propriety) - Social rites and etiquette as the fabric of civilization
3. Filial Piety - Respect for parents and ancestors as moral foundation
4. Golden Rule (Negative Form) - "Do not do to others what you do not want done to yourself"
5. Analects - Collected sayings compiled by disciples after his death
6. Junzi (Gentleman Ideal) - The cultivated moral person over noble birth
7. Rectification of Names - Words must match reality for society to function
8. Meritocratic Government - Rulers should be chosen by virtue and learning, not birth
9. Education for All - "In education there should be no class distinction"
10. Five Relationships - Ruler-subject, father-son, husband-wife, elder-younger, friend-friend
