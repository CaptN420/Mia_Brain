The 10 Most Famous Platonic Contributions:
1. Theory of Forms - Eternal perfect ideas behind imperfect material things
2. The Republic - Philosopher-kings, justice, the ideal state
3. Allegory of the Cave - Knowledge vs illusion metaphor in Book VII
4. The Academy - Founded ~387 BC in Athens; first institution of higher learning in the West
5. Socratic Dialogues - Preserved and dramatized Socrates' method across 30+ dialogues
6. Theory of the Soul - Tripartite soul: reason, spirit, appetite
7. Anamnesis - Learning as recollection of pre-existing knowledge
8. Timaeus - Demiurge cosmology shaping medieval science and theology
9. Platonic Love - Ascending love from beauty of bodies to Beauty itself
10. Mathematical Idealism - "Let no one ignorant of geometry enter" inscribed at Academy door
