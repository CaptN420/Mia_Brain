The 10 Most Famous Works & Ideas of Leonardo da Vinci:
1. Mona Lisa - Most famous painting in the world, sfumato technique, Louvre collection
2. The Last Supper - Milan mural, perspective mastery, emotional composition
3. Vitruvian Man - Human proportions drawing, symbol of art meeting science
4. Flying Machines - Ornithopter sketches, aerodynamics studied centuries before flight
5. Anatomical Studies - Human dissections, heart and fetus drawings, 240+ anatomy sheets
6. The Notebooks - 7,000+ pages of mirror-script observations spanning art, science, engineering
7. Hydraulic Engineering - Canal systems, water dynamics studies for Milan and Florence
8. Military Engineering - Tank design, fortifications, siege machines for Ludovico Sforza
9. Optics and Light Studies - Camera obscura experiments, shadow and light theory for painting
10. Empirical Method - Experiment-based inquiry before the scientific method existed
