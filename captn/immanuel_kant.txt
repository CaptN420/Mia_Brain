The 10 Most Famous Kantian Contributions:
1. Categorical Imperative - Act only on maxims you could will as universal law
2. Critique of Pure Reason - Synthesis of rationalism and empiricism; transcendental idealism
3. Copernican Revolution of Mind - Mind structures experience rather than passively receiving it
4. Humanity as End - Treat persons always as ends, never merely as means
5. Phenomena vs Noumena - We know appearances, never things-in-themselves
6. Categorical Duty Ethics - Moral worth comes from duty, not consequences
7. Perpetual Peace - Blueprint for international federation and democratic peace theory
8. Aesthetics - Critique of Judgment; disinterested beauty and sublime
9. Autonomy of Reason - Enlightenment as emergence from self-imposed immaturity
10. Influence - Foundation of modern ethics, deontology, and human dignity in law
